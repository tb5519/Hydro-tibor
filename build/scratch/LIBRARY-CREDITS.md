# Built-in Scratch library assets

These sprites, costumes, backdrops and sounds originate from Scratch and its
contributors. The catalog is taken from the pinned TurboWarp scratch-gui source
(see `build-manifest.json` and `library-assets/manifest.json`). Original catalog
names, contributor tags and metadata remain in the corresponding source archive.
The mirrored asset bytes are unchanged; their original MD5 filenames and a
SHA-256 release manifest identify every file.

Scratch's reuse guidance describes most support materials as Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike, with specific trademark exceptions:
https://mitscratch.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/4000156892-may-i-use-remix-scratch-support-materials-sprites-images-sounds-or-sample-projects-i-ve-found-on

Scratch and its contributors retain their applicable copyrights and attribution.
This mirror does not grant additional rights or permission to use Scratch marks.
The pinned TurboWarp catalogs already omit the stock trademarked characters.
Retain this notice, the upstream notices and the corresponding catalog metadata
when redistributing this library. OneByOne is not an official Scratch service.

The separate TurboWarp GUI, Scratch renderer and dependency source licenses are
preserved in `UPSTREAM-LICENSE`, `UPSTREAM-README.md`, `UPSTREAM-TRADEMARK` and the
corresponding source archive; they do not replace the artwork licenses.
